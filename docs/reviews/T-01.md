# Review register: T-01 — Flaky "withdraws an offer" browser test

- Risk tier: normal (`apps/web/src/pages/QuotePage.tsx`)
- Status: complete (round 1 against `main`: Codex approved, no findings)

Cause (found 2026-11-09, before any review): every list of bids and offers (`/quotes…`) keeps its last answer and shows it at once while it loads again (`apps/web/src/lib/useApiResource.ts`). After a withdrawal, the Bids & offers list loaded earlier in the test still showed the offer as live until its new answer arrived; on a slow CI run that took over 5 seconds (run #331). The same happened after new terms. Not only the test's fault: a person saw the old list for a moment too. Fix: the quote page forgets the kept `/quotes` answers after a withdrawal or new terms.
